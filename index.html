How it plays

Rules & game loop

Clear competitive systems buyers can demo instantly — campaign rivals + stake PvP.

PvP matchmaking

  1. Find an opponent near your level (or slightly higher).
  2. Pay stake coins to start — Standard 1,000 → 2,000 or High 5,000 → 10,000.
  3. Turns are timed at 30 seconds.
  4. Win = double stake coins + a card reward (common / rare / epic odds scale with stake).
